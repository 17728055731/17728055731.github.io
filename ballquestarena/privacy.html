Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Nov 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how we gather, manage and safeguard user related information when you use BallQuestArena, a ball themed arena challenge mobile application (referred to as "the App"). By downloading, installing, launching or interacting with this application, you affirm that you have read, understood, and accept all data handling practices described in this document. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please discontinue using the App. We may revise this statement from time to time and encourage you to review it regularly within App Settings.

1. Information We Gather

We adhere to data minimization principles and only collect information necessary to deliver stable arena based gameplay, maintain account functionality, strengthen anti abuse controls and enhance overall user satisfaction. The categories of collected information are set out below:

•                      Account Linked Profile Data: When you choose to sign in via authorized third party services including Apple ID or Google Sign In, we receive your validated email address, public display alias and user profile picture. This information is used exclusively to establish your player identity, create your arena game account and synchronize your arena match records, unlocked ball skins and personal preferences across multiple devices. We will not retrieve extra private data unrelated to account setup.

•                      Device Advertising Identifiers: We collect your device specific advertising identifier (IDFA for iOS, GAID for Android). These identifiers support anti fraud assessment and help our ad partners deliver relevant, non intrusive advertisements within the arena gameplay environment.

•                      Device and Network Technical Information: We capture your public IP address, hardware device model, operating system release version, screen display configuration, system default language and time zone setting. This technical dataset assists us in diagnosing network interruptions, adapting arena scene rendering for different hardware and spotting suspicious access patterns.

•                      Game Session Usage Metrics: We gather anonymous usage statistics reflecting your in app activity, including arena challenge attempts, match completion outcomes, item consumption frequency, feature interaction habits and overall session length. This aggregated data allows us to refine arena difficulty levels, adjust reward balancing and improve general gameplay quality.

•                      Fault and Performance Diagnostic Data: If the App suffers freezing, crashing or asset loading failures, runtime logs and error snapshots will be collected automatically. This material helps our development team trace technical defects and boost application stability.

•                      Optional Installed App List Permission: Upon initial launch, integrated advertising SDK may request permission to view applications installed on your device. You hold full authority to deny this permission prompt. Rejection will not interfere with core arena challenge gameplay features. Our development team cannot access or store this application list; all related processing is managed independently by the corresponding advertising service provider under its own privacy terms.

2. How We Utilize Your Collected Information

All gathered information shall only be processed for legitimate service connected purposes listed in this section. We will never repurpose your data for unrelated commercial activities unless you provide separate explicit consent.

•                      Preserve continuous arena game operation and synchronize your match history, unlocked content and saved user settings across your logged in devices.

•                      Optimize arena scene rendering, touch response performance and hardware compatibility for various smartphone and tablet devices.

•                      Facilitate appropriate in app advertisements and reward driven gameplay tasks in cooperation with advertising identifiers supplied by your device.

•                      Receive, categorize and reply to user feedback, gameplay suggestion submissions and technical fault report messages sent via support channels.

•                      Issue in app notifications covering new arena events, limited time skin releases, service maintenance announcements and updates to this Privacy Policy.

•                      Detect fake accounts, improper reward claiming behaviour and automated abusive operations in order to maintain fair playing conditions for all arena participants.

3. Circumstances Where Information May Be Shared

We respect user privacy and will never sell, lease or trade your personally identifiable information for third party marketing purposes. Information sharing is limited to the following compliant scenarios:

•                      Your advertising identifier may be shared with certified ad service partners for advertisement rendering, exposure statistics and rewarded task fulfilment. All cooperating vendors are bound by formal confidentiality obligations and are prohibited from re using this identifier for external business projects.

•                      Aggregated, non identifiable statistical datasets that cannot trace back to any individual user may be passed to third party analytical organizations for product iteration and gameplay experience improvement.

•                      Authorized service suppliers such as cloud hosting operators and crash log analysis platforms may access limited operational data required to carry out their assigned duties, and must comply with our defined privacy protection standards.

•                      We may disclose the minimal required set of user information in response to valid legal demands such as court orders, official regulatory investigations or statutory compliance requirements.

4. Data Security Measures

We implement multi layer technical and administrative safeguards to protect your stored and transmitted user information against unauthorized access, tampering, leakage and loss:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile device and our backend servers is secured via TLS encryption. Persistently stored personal account records are protected using high strength encryption standards.

•                      Granular internal access controls are enforced; only designated staff with genuine business process requirements may view sensitive user data, and all such personnel have signed formal confidentiality undertakings.

•                      We run periodic security inspections, vulnerability scanning and system upgrade procedures to address evolving cybersecurity risks.

Please note that no electronic storage or internet transmission technology can guarantee absolute data security. While we take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information, complete safety cannot be guaranteed for data travelling over public networks.

5. Data Retention Rules

Your information will only be retained for the minimal period necessary to fulfil collection objectives or satisfy applicable legal requirements:

•                      Your account related details, arena match archives and profile content remain stored while your account stays active. After your valid deletion application is approved, these personally identifiable datasets will be erased immediately.

•                      Anonymous gameplay session metrics and technical diagnostic logs will be held for no longer than 12 months, after which records will be anonymized or permanently deleted.

•                      Certain non identifiable anti fraud markers may be retained for a short monitoring cycle to counter reward abuse behaviour and will be cleared once risk assessment work concludes.

•                      Data subject to mandatory legal archiving obligations will be preserved until those statutory obligations expire.

6. Account and Associated Data Deletion Request

You hold the permanent right to apply for full erasure of your BallQuestArena account plus all linked user related data at any time, using one of the two methods below:

  1.                 In App Self Service: Open BallQuestArena, navigate to Settings > Account & Privacy, select the account deletion option and follow on screen prompts to submit your verified deletion application. Identity validation with your registered email address may be required.
  1.                 Email Application: Send an email to our support inbox with subject line: BallQuestArena Full Account and Data Deletion Request. Please include your registered account email for identity verification purposes.

Our support team will complete review and processing within 30 calendar days and issue a confirmation notice once deletion finishes. Please keep in mind that after removal, all arena match progress, unlocked skins, reward balances and account profile information cannot be recovered.

8. Privacy Protection for Minors

BallQuestArena is built for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information belonging to children under the age of 13. Should we accidentally obtain minor related personal data, we will promptly remove all such records from our server infrastructure. If you are a parent or legal guardian and discover your under age child has submitted personal information to us, please reach out via our support email to apply for complete data removal.
